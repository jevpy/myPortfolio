## Scope and decisions
- Ordered default candidates: `openai/gpt-oss-20b`, `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, `qwen/qwen3.8-27b` (preview explicitly included).
- An authorized configured primary moves first; an unknown custom model retains single-model behavior.
- Model-specific cooldowns and bounded sequential attempts, max three distinct candidates per request; normal AI response has no client-wide cooldown. Skip unavailable models; retry the principal on a later eligible question. No background retry loop.
- Shared/ambiguous quota, auth/access failures, malformed/unsafe output and network/timeout failures stop safely; no attempt to bypass account limits. Fail over only on positively identified model-scoped errors.
- Preserve one in-flight sequence and at most four **provider starts**, not visitor requests, per rolling minute per warm worker. Warm-worker state is neither persistent nor global enforcement.
- One total eight-second handler budget including parsing and provider attempts. Cancel error/late bodies and maintain size bounds, grounding, no-store and response schema.
- GPT-OSS uses `include_reasoning:false`; Qwen uses `reasoning_format:'hidden'` per https://console.groq.com/docs/reasoning (mutually exclusive). Do not expose reasoning or provider error bodies.
- Three model quotas are not guaranteed additive; no 1,000-response promise. AI remains disabled until private owner configuration.
